📖 Guide

How to Install

Up and running in under 60 seconds.

1

Open Roblox Studio

Launch your own game or a private place in Roblox Studio where you have full permissions.

2

Create a LocalScript

In the Explorer panel, right-click StarterPlayerScripts and insert a LocalScript.

3

Paste the Code

Copy the full TrollScript.lua from the repository and paste it into your new LocalScript.

4

Hit Play & Enjoy Chaos

Press F5 to playtest. The green-and-magenta GUI appears in the top-left — toggle any mode and watch the mayhem unfold.
